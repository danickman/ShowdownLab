## One substantive physically local mechanic
An L5 Goblin lands a **real positive HP stab**, and releases a bounded short-lived smoke screen around its own physical contact point. This grants 76% damage mitigation (incoming ×0.24) to that single Goblin and up to two closest living allied Goblins within 0.16 arena units for 52 ticks, with an 80-tick per-attacker recharge. The effect is never global, carries no new HP or direct damage, creates no imaginary attacks or recursion, never alters positions, target scores, speed, stun, dodge randomness, or other roles, and doesn't reach detached fights. If a member dies, its smoke benefit ends with its body. L1–L4 exact physics remain unchanged. All damage still passed through ordinary core hit processing. Source-only VM injection in tools/qa/check-goblin-l5-smoke-veil-roi-turn13.cjs; at this study stage playable v3/ultimate-abilities.js untouched.
