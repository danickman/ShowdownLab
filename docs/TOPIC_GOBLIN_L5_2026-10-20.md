## New mechanism: physically triggered enemy disruption
Prior damage scalars, accelerated self stabbing and increasingly powerful blood healing all failed to achieve consistent L5 win ROI. Change the nature of this evolved fighter: an L5 Goblin reaching and actually injuring a living enemy triggers at most one **Trick Ambush** each 92 ticks, affecting that hit victim plus at most the closest two geographically nearby alive opponents within 0.17.
- **Pocket Dust:** max 24-tick stun on those up to 3, bounded to a true stabbed victim and no direct damage. Cannot jump detached fronts.
- **Sabotage:** mark those up to 3 for 88 ticks; while still exposed, subsequent real incoming ordinary/spell hits deal 1.75x after other deterministic mitigation. No new direct attack or free remote damaging events; only existing physical contacts resolve HP. No tier1–4 or other-role changes.
