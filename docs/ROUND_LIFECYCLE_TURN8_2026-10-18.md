## Demonstrated lifecycle defect and narrow production fix

The `ShowdownAdapter.applyDraft()` existing source guarded one-round disruption behind `phase==='round_over'`, but **allowed add/mult/up from idle, while active combat, after match_over, and multiple times between two rounds**. That could mutate persistent roster/level state midbattle and permit duplicate picks; a transition bug distinct from deterministic physical combat. Fix gates **all** draft kinds behind `round_over` and enforces exactly **one accepted choice per team per round**. Keep separate team flags so human Blue and bot Red can each make their normal one pick. A malformed rejected choice does not consume the allowance. Reset flags atomically on deploy, hard reset and stop. Already-existing match-instance battleId still gates old `onState` callbacks; no change to source combat physics, target priority, real HP, 300 adapter or x2 projected 120 cap.

`tools/qa/check-round-lifecycle-guards.cjs` uses the genuine unmodified Evolution rule and new Adapter code with deterministic scripted core terminal callbacks, supplementing—not substituting—the real 300-fighter and full-match suites. Asserts: four draft kinds invalid in idle and battle; duplicate terminal callbacks cannot double deduct lives; after a valid blue pick, second pick fails while red AI can accept one; next round resets picks; late prior-id callback cannot mutate new match; valid next-battle Jam/Thin auto clears and never changes permanent army/evolution; stop/reset discards stale sources; a fresh battle ID is monotonic and rejects old callbacks; match-over cannot accept draft or nextRound; all emitted states finite and coherent. Existing normal human/bot round-loop calls were checked for phase compatibility, and `tools/qa/check-round-winner-lives.cjs` preserved.
